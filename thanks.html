List Your Own · 450 W Schaumburg Rd, Schaumburg, IL 60195 · (779) 800-9303 · Listing nationwide — 1% on homes up to $1,000,000, 1.5% over $1,000,000

The listing fee — 1% on homes priced up to $1,000,000 and 1.5% on homes priced over $1,000,000, calculated on the final sale price — is a listing-side, limited-service (listing-only) commission. It does not include compensation to a buyer's brokerage or agent, which the seller offers separately and pays at closing if a buyer's agent is involved. All commissions are negotiable; there is no standard or set commission rate. Terms are confirmed in a written listing agreement before your home is listed.
